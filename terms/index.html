1. The service, account and merchant profiles

Vendora is an Android workspace for merchants. Depending on the release and your configuration, it can process supported payment SMS on the phone, run USSD workflows through the phone and SIM, keep customer and transaction records, and provide reporting and account services. Feature availability depends on the app version, Android and device behaviour, carrier, SIM, permissions, connectivity, account state and any connected provider.

One Firebase sign-in is used for the account identity and can be associated with up to five merchant profile slots. A profile slot is not a separate account or a separate person. Select the intended merchant profile before handling records. Session and installation controls may limit which signed-in devices can act for an active profile. Your sign-in does not make a record on one device a verified record on another.

2. Device use, permissions and local workflows

SMS intake and USSD automation run on the Android device and depend on Android permissions, the selected SIM, carrier menus, network response and the phone remaining available. You decide whether to grant permissions and can change them in Android settings. If a permission or carrier feature is unavailable, a related workflow may stop or require manual handling. Review the amount, recipient, SIM and result before relying on a transaction. Do not assume that a queued, timed-out or unclear result means that a payment or delivery succeeded or failed.

Vendora displays time values as absolute date and time values with the applicable time-zone context where available. Your phone's clock, time zone, network and service timestamps can differ. Keep independent receipts and business records where required. A displayed time or status is not a substitute for the payment provider's authoritative confirmation.

3. Local processing, cloud records and backups

Some SMS, transaction, customer and workflow data is stored and processed locally on the device so that supported merchant work can continue without internet access. Selected account, profile, session, activity, service-payment and online-order records use Firebase cloud services. Those are separate processing paths: a local transaction or receipt does not automatically become a server order, and a server order does not prove that the phone completed an offline USSD action.

Local merchant histories are not a guaranteed cloud backup. Offline changes can remain only on that phone and can be lost if it is damaged, reset or removed. Do not rely on Vendora as the only copy of a legally or commercially important record. When connectivity returns, any supported sync may take time; avoid repeating an action until you have checked its status and supporting receipt.

Runs, Time and optional unit backups

Runs are consumable app execution units. Time access has the expiry or duration disclosed at purchase. Neither represents money held for withdrawal, airtime float, a bank balance or a domain renewal. A website subscription is maintained separately on the server and does not need a local-unit backup.

Where Unit backup is enabled, an eligible signed-in profile may back up Runs, remaining Time, or both. Creating a usable backup requires the source phone to clear the selected balance and the server to acknowledge that operation. A pending backup is not proof that both steps finished. Remaining Time is paused while held in this specific backup. Its expiry deadline is fixed using the policy in force when it is created; later policy changes do not extend an existing backup. An expired or revoked unused backup cannot be redeemed. Codes are confidential, account-bound and single-use; eligible profiles under the same account can restore them. Account-wide outstanding-type limits and weekly creation limits are shown in the app.

Normal logout and supported remote device handover require internet access and an acknowledged source logout before the replacement phone can use the profile. These flows transfer eligible units and stop source automation. Time keeps its original expiry during logout or handover; it is not paused as it is in Unit backup. Do not clear app data or uninstall while a balance transfer is pending. Support may inspect retained acknowledgement and audit records to resolve interrupted transfers, without issuing a second copy of the same balance.
