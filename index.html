A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you get started.

Do I need my own domain for the Android app?

No. A domain is for a merchant website. The Android app has its own account and merchant setup.

Can website buyers send airtime to a different number?

The planned website checkout keeps the payment phone separate from the airtime recipient. Merchant online checkout will open after the provider connection is ready and tested.

How does the website rate work?

At a 90% selling rate, KES 1,000 of airtime costs the buyer KES 900. Rates support two decimal places; fractional payment shillings are dropped. The final price is calculated by the backend.

What if an M-PESA package payment is delayed?

Check the same purchase in the app. If M-PESA debited you, do not submit another payment while confirmation is pending. Contact support if it remains unresolved.
