VENDORA QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Airtime automation FAQ.

Learn what Vendora does, how to set up your Android phone, how execution works and what to expect from accounts, units and merchant websites.

Getting started

What is Vendora?

Vendora is an Android airtime selling automation app for merchants in Kenya. It combines supported payment SMS intake, configured SIM-based USSD execution, customer records, transaction history and business statistics. The merchant controls the phone, SIM, payment setup and execution settings.

Who is Vendora for?

Vendora is for airtime sellers and businesses that want to manage payment messages and configured airtime workflows on an Android phone. It is a merchant tool, rather than a consumer airtime shop on this homepage.

How does airtime selling automation work?

A supported payment SMS is processed on the phone and recorded as a transaction. Vendora checks the configured merchant, execution settings and readiness before running the SIM-based USSD workflow. You can review the resulting status and history in the app; receiving a payment message alone does not prove airtime delivery.

Does Vendora work on iPhone or in a browser?

The airtime automation app runs on Android and uses Android SMS, SIM and accessibility capabilities. This public website explains the product and links to official Android releases. It does not run your phone's USSD workflow in a browser, and no iPhone automation app is offered here.

Where do I download the official Vendora app?

Use the official download section on this website. It checks stable APK releases in the official VendoraWeb GitHub repository and shows a download only when a release is available. If no release is available, contact support; avoid unofficial APK links.

Phone setup and execution

Does Vendora process SMS and USSD on my phone?

Vendora uses Android capabilities for SMS intake and USSD automation. You control Android permissions and can change or revoke them in your device settings. Local app records may be kept on the device to support your workflow.

Which permissions does Vendora need?

Depending on the feature, Vendora needs SMS access for supported payment messages, phone and SIM access for dialing, and its accessibility service to interact with configured USSD dialogs. Notification and background settings help you follow activity and keep the engine available. Review the in-app permission guide and enable only the features you intend to use.

What are the Till, Number and PayBill execution modes?

The allowed execution modes are Till, Number and PayBill. Till covers the configured till-to-till workflow, Number covers number-to-till, and PayBill covers number-to-paybill. These are execution settings for the merchant workflow; they are not a promise that every network or payment account is supported.

Can I allow only one execution mode?

Yes. In Settings, select at least one of Till, Number or PayBill. You can enable one, two or all three. Vendora blocks execution for modes you have not enabled; those transactions are marked DISALLOWED.

What happens when I turn automation off?

Turning automation off prevents new automatic executions. Review the status of any transaction already in progress before taking another action. The green play button starts automation; the red stop button turns it off.

Why is a transaction held or DISALLOWED?

DISALLOWED means its execution mode is not enabled in your settings. A held transaction may be waiting for automation, accessibility, valid account authorization or available units. Read the transaction's status and history, resolve the stated reason, and review the payment and carrier result before retrying.

Can I retry a failed or uncertain transaction?

Use the recovery actions available for that transaction after checking its history and the carrier or payment confirmation. An uncertain result may already have caused a deduction or delivery, so repeating it without checking can duplicate the purchase. Contact support if the recorded result and the provider receipt disagree.

Does Vendora need internet and an active phone?

SIM-based SMS and USSD work runs on the merchant's Android phone, which must be powered on with the necessary permissions and mobile service available. Internet is required for account and connected cloud operations, including supported unit purchases, backups, device transfers and website services. Do not assume every feature works offline or after Android stops the app.

Accounts, units and records

Can one Vendora account manage multiple merchant profiles?

One Firebase sign-in can be associated with up to five merchant profile slots. Each slot is a business profile under the same account, not a separate login. Select the intended profile before reviewing records or configuring execution, and follow the app's device and session controls.

What are Runs and Time?

Runs are consumable app execution units. Time gives access for the duration or expiry shown at purchase. Neither is airtime float, customer money or a withdrawable balance. Website subscriptions are separate. Current plans, limits and purchase details are shown inside Vendora.

What does a Unit backup save?

Where available, Unit backup transfers selected Runs, remaining Time or both into an account-bound, single-use backup after the source phone clears the selected balance and the server acknowledges it. Remaining Time is paused in this specific backup. Check its displayed expiry and limits in the app. A Unit backup does not back up your customer list or transaction history.

Can I move Vendora to another phone?

Use the supported logout or device handover flow with internet access and wait for the source logout acknowledgement before using the profile on the replacement phone. Eligible units can transfer, but Time keeps its original expiry during this flow. Do not uninstall or clear source app data while a transfer is pending.

How do I reset my password or verify my email?

For an email-and-password account, use Forgot password on the Vendora sign-in screen or request a reset link from your account settings. Open the newest Firebase account-action email to complete the reset. For verification, use the verification email or request a fresh link in the app. Google sign-in accounts use Google's account recovery. Never share passwords or one-time action links with support.

What information is stored online?

Account authentication and merchant or service records used by Vendora may be stored in Firebase cloud services. The app uses these records to provide account and connected services. See the Privacy Policy for details and contact support to ask about a record request.

Are my transaction records automatically backed up to the cloud?

Local customer and transaction histories are not a guaranteed cloud backup. Some account and service records are stored online, but important phone records may remain only on that device. Use the relevant export or backup options where available and keep your own copies of important business records.

Merchant websites and safety

Can I connect my own domain to a merchant website?

Vendora supports merchant website setup connected to a merchant account, subject to service readiness. Keep the domain registered in your own account and retain renewal control. Ask support about the current setup process, provider requirements and available website features before paying for a connection.

Is live online airtime checkout available?

The eTop production fulfillment adapter is currently disabled pending provider integration and validation. A draft website or saved provider credentials do not mean live eTop checkout or delivery is available. This is separate from Vendora's on-phone SMS and USSD automation. Ask support about current online service availability.

How do merchant websites handle credentials?

Connection credentials supplied for an online merchant service are kept in server-only records and are not published on this website. Website service availability and payment processing depend on the connected providers.

Are website renewals included?

No. A merchant website's monthly service and the domain registration renewal are separate. Domain renewal is paid to the registrar that holds your domain.

Does Vendora ask for my M-PESA PIN?

No. Vendora does not need or request your M-PESA PIN. Never send it to support or enter it into a website credentials form. Authorized online integrations may require separate provider credentials, which are handled as private server-side configuration.

Is Vendora an official Safaricom or M-PESA app?

Vendora is an independently operated merchant tool under LYCO SERVICES, with parent LYCO TECHNOLOGIES. It is not presented as a Safaricom or M-PESA app or as an endorsement by those providers. Carrier and payment services remain subject to their own terms and availability.

How do I contact Vendora support?

Email support@myvendora.co.ke or use WhatsApp support. Describe the issue and relevant transaction status while hiding private customer information. Do not send passwords, backup codes, M-PESA PINs or account-action links.

Where can I read the terms?

See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Contact us if you need help understanding a feature or your account records.
