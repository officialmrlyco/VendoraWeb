1. Who is responsible for processing

For Vendora account identity, app operation, platform subscriptions, service security and support, LYCO SERVICES determines why and how those records are used and acts as the data controller for those purposes. When a merchant uses Vendora to collect or manage information about that merchant's customers, the merchant usually decides the business purpose and is responsible for its customer notice and lawful basis. Vendora may process that information on the merchant's instructions to provide the service. A provider may separately act as a controller for its own security, network, payment or legal purposes. The role depends on the actual processing and applicable law.

This public notice does not state that the operator is registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), or that any particular registration exemption applies. The operator must determine and meet any registration or other compliance obligation that applies to it.

2. Information handled and its source

  • Account and profile details: Firebase Authentication user identifier, name and email, verification/sign-in state, merchant or business name, merchant contact phone and email, profile settings, and the selected merchant profile. One Firebase identity can be associated with up to five merchant profile slots.
  • Phone and transaction records: supported incoming SMS content used for payment intake, transaction details, customer name or phone where present, and locally generated processing status, history and activity. SMS intake and USSD execution happen on the phone. Some selected account, session, transaction, synchronization or service records may also be sent to Firebase; not every SMS or local record is automatically uploaded.
  • Online order and payment information: merchant site application and configuration details, domain, order and subscription identifiers, amount and status, payment reference and payer phone, and an airtime recipient phone. The payer and recipient may be different people. Some order views mask contact details. Platform subscription payments to Vendora and any merchant customer order payments are separate records and payment flows.
  • Merchant credentials: a merchant who sets up a connected online service may provide Daraja credentials and account details such as a Consumer Key and Secret, passkey, shortcode, Till or PayBill destination; an optional eTop key may be saved for later setup. These credentials are stored in server-only records and are not returned to the app as secret values or shown on the public storefront. Vendora does not request or need an M-PESA PIN. Do not send one to us or put it in a form or message.
  • Device and activity details: generated app installation identifier, merchant session version, device name/model/manufacturer/type, app version and build number, and event type and timestamp such as sign-in, sign-out, app open, online status or automation state. Units, expiry times, rate changes and device handover acknowledgements support account/session security, service operation and activity reporting. Approved administrators can review these records and bounded payment/backup histories to manage the service and investigate disputes.
  • Unit backups and transfers: selected Runs or remaining Time, backup identifier/code, creation and fixed expiry, source/destination profile and installation, redemption/revocation status and acknowledgements. A server audit trail can remain after a code is used so duplicate redemption and interrupted transfers can be investigated. A backup code is confidential; do not share it outside the account.
  • Support and legal records: messages and attachments you choose to send, request and response records, policy version/links and acceptance time where recorded, and information needed to resolve a payment, security or legal issue.
  • Website connection information: a browser's network address, request details and basic technical information may be processed by the public website host, DNS/network provider and GitHub when the page or release data is requested.

3. Why we use information and lawful grounds

We use information to create and protect accounts; operate requested app and merchant features; run local SMS and USSD workflows; keep selected cloud records, sessions and synchronization; set up and review merchant websites; administer subscriptions and payment confirmation; respond to support and rights requests; investigate errors, abuse and security incidents; and meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Depending on the data and purpose, processing relies on a lawful ground available under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, including steps needed to provide a service you request or perform an agreement, your consent where required, compliance with a legal obligation, or a legitimate interest that is not overridden by your rights. We assess the ground for the particular use. Where consent is the ground, you may withdraw it for future processing; withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. Android permission settings control access to phone features, but a device permission is not a general consent to unrelated uses of personal data.

Vendora does not use transaction or customer records for unrelated advertising on this public site. If we send direct marketing based on personal data in the future, it will require the consent and clear opt-out mechanism required by law, and service or security messages will be distinguished from marketing.

4. Service providers, disclosure and cross-border processing

  • Firebase and Google: account authentication and selected cloud records, session controls, service operation and payment confirmation.
  • GitHub Pages and GitHub: hosting the public website and serving the release metadata requested by its download section.
  • Cloudflare: DNS and, where configured for a connected site, network or storefront delivery.
  • Mobile operators and Safaricom: phone, SIM, SMS, USSD and mobile-payment transport. Safaricom Daraja handles applicable M-PESA online payment requests and confirmations.
  • Other transaction providers: a provider receives information needed for the specific service or payment flow it operates and processes it under its own terms and privacy information. The eTop production adapter is currently disabled, so Vendora does not send live customer orders to eTop through that adapter.

We may disclose information to a provider or professional adviser that needs it to deliver a service, to an authority where law requires it, or where reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, systems or the service. We do not publish private merchant account, transaction or credential records on this marketing website.

Providers may process data in countries other than Kenya. We do not promise that all data is stored in Kenya or identify a country unless the applicable provider configuration has been verified. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, the operator must use a transfer ground and safeguards required by the Data Protection Act, including any additional safeguards required for sensitive personal data. Provider terms and technical configurations can change; contact us to ask about the safeguards relevant to a particular service.

5. The public website, cookies and Turnstile

The public marketing site has no account sign-in for merchant records, credential upload form, analytics, advertising tracker, cookie storage or Turnstile challenge in its current code. The download section requests official VendoraWeb release metadata from GitHub when it loads. The separate account-email action page handles a one-time Firebase email link and communicates with Firebase Authentication to complete that action; do not share the link or its one-time code.

The website host or a security/network provider may process connection information or use technical measures needed to deliver and protect a request. Links to email, WhatsApp, GitHub or other providers take you to those services, which apply their own privacy terms and technologies. If Vendora later adds cookies, Turnstile or analytics to its own pages, this notice and any required choices will be updated before that feature is used.

The separate private administrator portal uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in persistence and Cloudflare Turnstile for bot verification. These providers process the technical request/browser information necessary for those functions. Server sign-in limits use hashed identifiers derived from an account email or network address, request counts and expiry timestamps; administrator session and password-reset request records support access control and abuse prevention. Application passwords and Turnstile response tokens are not retained in Vendora's rate-limit records. The public homepage does not use this administrator login flow.

6. Retention, backups and security

Personal data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, applicable legal duties, service operation, security, support or a dispute. Retention differs between the phone, Firebase, payment/network providers and any backups. A local record may remain on the device after a cloud change, and backups or provider copies may not change at the same time. Local merchant histories are not a guaranteed cloud backup. There is no single fixed retention period or deletion time for every record stated in this notice.

A Unit backup's displayed expiry controls redemption eligibility, rather than promising deletion of its audit trail at that instant. Restored balances and completed transfers use acknowledgements to prevent repeated application. Account closure and valid deletion requests are assessed against applicable record-retention duties and exceptions; we restrict retained data to the purpose that justifies keeping it.

We use access controls and service-specific security measures intended to protect information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and account credentials protected, do not share payment secrets, and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

7. Data rights and how to make a request

Subject to the conditions and limits in applicable law, a data subject may ask to be informed about use of their data, access it, object to processing, correct false or misleading information, request deletion or erasure where the legal conditions are met, request restriction where available, withdraw consent where consent is the ground, and exercise any applicable right to data portability or protection from solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. A right may not apply in every case, and lawful retention or other statutory exceptions may apply.

Send a request to support@myvendora.co.ke with the subject “Privacy request”. You may also contact Vendora support on WhatsApp. Tell us the account or data concerned and the right you wish to exercise. We may ask for proportionate information to verify identity and authority. For information a merchant controls about its customers, contact that merchant first; you may also contact us and we will route the request appropriately where we can. We will handle a valid request within the time and process required by law. A local copy may need to be removed on the phone itself.

You may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if you believe your data-protection rights have been infringed. Nothing in this notice requires you to contact us before approaching the ODPC. The ODPC publishes information on data-subject rights and how to file a complaint.

8. Security incidents

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will assess and document it, take reasonable containment and recovery steps, and notify the ODPC and affected people within the time and in the circumstances required by Kenyan law. If a provider processes data for a merchant, the merchant and Vendora will follow the applicable controller/processor notice duties and incident-response terms.

9. Children and information handled for a merchant

Vendora accounts and merchant tools are intended for adults conducting business, not for use by children under 18 as account holders. The app may incidentally process a child's contact detail if it appears in a merchant's customer record or message. Merchants must not submit or use children's personal data unless they have a lawful basis and have met any parent/guardian, child-protection and consent duties that apply. Contact us if you believe a child's data has been provided without authority so we can assess the request and applicable law.

If you are a merchant, you are responsible for deciding what customer data is necessary, giving your customers a clear privacy notice, obtaining consent or another lawful basis where required, responding to requests about records you control, limiting access in your business and not using information for a different purpose without authority. Use Vendora's customer information only for the business purpose disclosed to the person and in line with applicable law.

For privacy questions or requests to LYCO SERVICES, contact support@myvendora.co.ke or WhatsApp support. This notice may change when product features, providers or law change. The current version and effective date are shown at the top of this page.
