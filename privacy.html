Information on this website

This website has no account registration, payment form or merchant credential upload. It reads public package settings from Firebase and official release information from GitHub. Those services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address when your browser makes a request.

Hosting and links

The site uses GitHub Pages with Cloudflare domain services. These providers may process connection and security logs under their own policies. WhatsApp, email and GitHub download links open external services. Their privacy policies apply when you use them.

Support

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send your M-PESA PIN or raw API secrets over public contact channels.

The Vendora app and merchant services
